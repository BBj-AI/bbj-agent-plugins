<objective>
Decide, implement and record the CONTENT_VERSION policy of the release-text gates (REL-06,
decisions D-04 to D-07). The generic gate `upgrade_addressed` runs at every version. The two fact
gates `upgrade_quotes` and `not_run` stay pinned to CONTENT_VERSION. At any other version they FAIL
with a message that says to re-pin the facts, and they never print skip. The fact values stay at
0.2.0 (D-06).

Purpose: today the three content gates print `skip` at every version other than 0.2.0. At 0.2.1
that would block the skip=0 release run of Phase 10 (REL-08), or let a release pass with stale
facts. After this plan a version bump goes red until the new release writes its own facts.

Output: a reshaped `tests/test_release_text.py` with its policy docstring, four later-version
mutation cases in `tests/test_release_text_mutation.sh` (17 gates in total), and one Key Decisions
row in `.planning/PROJECT.md`.
</objective>
