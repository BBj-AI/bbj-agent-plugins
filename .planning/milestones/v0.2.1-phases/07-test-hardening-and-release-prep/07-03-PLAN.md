<objective>
Make `real_home_untouched` pass while a Codex daemon writes its runtime files, and still fail on
any change to Codex config, hooks or the installed skills (MAINT-07, the deferred 0.2.0 item D-2,
decisions D-08 to D-10). The gate stops comparing mtimes over the whole of `~/.codex` and
`~/.agents`. Instead it compares content checksums of exactly the paths the installer can write.
The same helper is proven in both directions on fake homes, in CI, without Codex.

Snapshot format (Claude's discretion under D-09): `F <crc> <size> <path>`, `D <path>`, `L <path>`,
`U <path>` and `- <path>`, sorted with LC_ALL=C. This is the prototype validated under dash, bash
and busybox sh on non-GNU find and cksum (07-RESEARCH.md Pattern 3).

Output: `home_snapshot` and `home_diff` in `tests/lib.sh`, the switched gate in
`tests/test_install_codex.sh`, and the new `tests/test_home_guard.sh` (13 gates, prefix
`homeguard_`).
</objective>
